Sarasota

A fictional Gulf-coast city.

Map data

© OpenStreetMap contributors

The street layout, building footprints, land-use areas and the bayfront water boundary of this district are derived from OpenStreetMap data, used under the Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0. See openstreetmap.org/copyright.

Map data is baked at author time. The game does not fetch map data while running.

Naming

The street layout, building footprints and coastline are real downtown Sarasota, Florida, baked from OpenStreetMap. Street names, the city name and the bay are real, because they are factual public geography carried in that data.

Every business, shopfront, sign and logo in this game is invented. The buildings are authored massing standing on real footprints, not surveys of real premises: only 5.7% carry a height from the source data. No real business is depicted, and any resemblance to one is unintended.

Assets

Every texture, mesh, animation and sound in this game is generated procedurally in code at runtime. No third-party art, model, or audio assets are used.
